    Questions

    Is this product video maker really free?

    Yes. Making and downloading videos is free: every template, the 4 free themes, your own colours, music, captions, the AI voice and recording your own voice. There's no sign-up and no watermark. Optional Motion Packs (paid themes with their own motion) are US$3 each, or US$5 for all 8 (one-time).

    Is there a watermark?

    No. There's an optional end card that says "Made free at leymish.com", and you can switch it off before you make the video. Only a preview made with a locked Motion Pack is stamped.

    Can I make a Black Friday video for free?

    Yes. Pick the Black Friday sale template, type your discount, what it covers, your code and the deadline, and download a 9:16 or 16:9 MP4. There's also a Christmas gift guide template with three products.

    Can it make a video from my product link?

    Yes. Paste a product page and it fills in the name, price, short points and up to 3 photos from the page's public product details. It works on most Shopify and WooCommerce product pages; some sites block automatic visits, and then you type the details yourself.

    Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

    No. Your photos, your logo, your recording and the finished video stay on your device. The page draws and encodes the video in your browser.

    Which browsers work?

    Chrome and Edge on a computer or an Android phone. Safari 26 and Firefox on a computer may work. Firefox on Android can preview but not export.

    Is the voice a real person?

    No. It's an AI-generated voice (Kokoro) that runs on your device and is labelled as AI. There's no voice cloning. You can record your own voice instead, or use music only.

    What are Motion Packs?

    Eight paid themes, each with its own motion: transitions such as whip pans, glitch cuts and light leaks, moving text (pop, typewriter, gold foil, neon), product moves, price effects and particles like confetti and snow. The free themes have basic motion (fades and slides). They include Neon Pulse, Black Friday Blast, Holiday Glow and Luxury Noir, plus Match my logo, which builds a theme from your logo's colours. You can make a full preview with any of them; it's stamped until you unlock the theme, then we re-make it clean. Prices are in US dollars, so your bank may convert. The money keeps this video maker free and pays for better free features. One-time, no subscription, and if a theme doesn't work for you, we refund it (terms).
